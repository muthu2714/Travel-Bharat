Food & Festivals of India

Temple chariots, harvest feasts and desert fairs — scroll through India's calendar of celebration.

Diwali

Pan-India

The festival of lights — oil lamps, fireworks and family feasts mark the return of light over darkness.

Pushkar Camel Fair

Rajasthan

A desert fairground of camel trading, folk music and hot-air balloons beside a sacred lake.

Thrissur Pooram

Kerala

A grand temple festival with caparisoned elephants and thunderous percussion ensembles.

Rath Yatra

Odisha

Enormous wooden chariots carrying temple deities are pulled through Puri's streets by thousands.

Durga Puja

West Bengal

Elaborate pandals and clay idols honour the goddess Durga across Kolkata's neighbourhoods.

Hemis Festival

Ladakh

Masked monks perform ritual Cham dances in a Himalayan monastery courtyard.

Bonalu

Telangana

Women carry decorated pots of rice offerings in colourful processions honouring goddess Mahakali.

Onam

Kerala

A harvest festival of flower carpets, boat races and grand banana-leaf feasts.

Kumbh Mela

uttar Pradesh

The world's largest pilgrim gathering, where millions bathe at a sacred river confluence.

Hornbill Festival

Nagaland

Naga tribes gather for traditional dance, music and craft in a week-long cultural showcase.
